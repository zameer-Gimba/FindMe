No smartphone? No data? No problem.

This simulates a basic keypad phone. No GPS or internet is needed: the mobile network already knows which tower the phone is on.

  1. Dial *347*112# and press Call. Then choose the emergency: 1 Attack, 2 Fire, 3 Medical, 4 Accident, 5 Other.
  2. Fastest way: put the number of the emergency before the #, and the alert goes at once. For example *347*112*2# sends a fire alert.
  3. Or send an SMS to 112 (free): HELP, TAIMAKO, EGBA MI or NYEM AKA. Add a word like FIRE, or reply with the number when asked.
  4. If the person's home was registered, responders also see the home address and directions.

Open the responder dashboard in another tab to watch alerts arrive.
