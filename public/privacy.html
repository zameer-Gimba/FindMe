Privacy notice

How FindMe handles personal data under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA).

FindMe is a prototype being evaluated. The responder dashboard is open for testing, so please only use test details and do not enter anyone's real personal information yet.

What we collect, and why

DataWhy we need it
Your location when you ask for help (GPS on a smartphone, or the cell-tower area for keypad phones)So responders can find you
Your phone numberSo responders can call you back, and to link a registered home
The kind of emergency and any SMS text you sendSo the right responders come
If you register a home: name (optional), home postcode, directions or landmark, who registered itSo responders know where you live when you call for help

We only use this data for emergency response. We never sell it or use it for advertising.

Your consent

  • We process your data with your consent. Registering a home needs a tick on the consent box and a YES reply by SMS from the phone itself.
  • Sending an alert shares your location with responders. That is the purpose of the alert.
  • You can withdraw consent at any time (see "Your rights").

Who can see it

  • Emergency and security responders, on the responder dashboard.
  • A registered home is only shown when that phone number sends an alert.
  • NIPOST's Postcode API receives GPS coordinates to turn them into a postcode. It does not receive names or phone numbers.
  • An SMS provider (Africa's Talking) delivers text messages, once real SMS is switched on.

How long we keep it

  • Alerts: deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Home registrations not confirmed by SMS: deleted after 7 days.
  • Confirmed homes: kept until you delete them.

Your rights

  • Delete your data: text DELETE to 112. We remove your home registration and your details from past alerts.
  • Say no to a registration: reply NO to the confirmation SMS and nothing is saved.
  • You can also ask to see or correct your data, or complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

Where it is stored

Data is stored on Netlify's servers, which may be outside Nigeria, and is protected in transit with HTTPS. A national deployment would use hosting approved by the relevant agencies.

Contact

Questions, or requests to see, correct or delete your data: email anmc@nticschools.org.
