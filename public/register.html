Register a home for emergencies

Most people don't know their postcode, and they don't need to. Stand at the house and we find it from GPS.

  1. Anyone with a smartphone can do it: the owner, a family member, the ward head, a health worker or a NIPOST agent.
  2. Enter the phone number the person uses, even a keypad phone.
  3. When that number calls for help, responders see this home and its directions.
🔒Private. Only security and emergency agencies can see this, and only when this number sends an alert.
